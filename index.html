FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to bring my own detergent?

You're welcome to bring your own, but you don't have to. We keep detergent, fabric softener, and dryer sheets available on site.

How do I pay for the machines?

Machines take quarters, and there's a change machine on site that breaks bills into quarters.

Can I wash comforters and large blankets?

Yes! Our 60 lb front-load washers easily handle king-size comforters, sleeping bags, and other bulky items.

How does wash & fold work?

Just drop off your laundry during business hours. We'll weigh it, wash it, dry it, and fold it. It's usually ready the same or next day.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's convenient parking right at the store on E Main St.
